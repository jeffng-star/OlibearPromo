Rebate rules

  • Open to dealers on our eligible list only. Your sales contact confirms this and your unit limit.
  • Covers fans bought from us before the campaign started, up to the stock you hold on the campaign start date. Send us your count on day one. Fans bought under the Year-End Fan Deal already carry the promo price.
  • Once you reach a tier, the rebate applies to every eligible fan you sold in the campaign, not just the extra ones.
  • Sell at RM 109 or lower. Fans sold above RM 109 do not qualify.
  • Send one claim with proof of sale after the campaign closes. We issue one credit note per dealer.
